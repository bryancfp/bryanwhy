WHY?

Hi. I'm Bryan. With a Y.

I have a habit of wondering why completely normal things are normal.

Why do we work five days a week? Why do restaurants expect us to calculate part of their employees' compensation? Why are there seven days in a week? Why do we shake hands? And why does somebody on the internet claim to have traveled through time every six months?

So I started looking into it.

Bryan With a Why is a collection of unnecessary investigations into ordinary things, strange history, human behavior, and questions nobody asked me to answer.
